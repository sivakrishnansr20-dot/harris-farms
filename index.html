Our Goat Breeds

Dairy Breed

Boer Goat

Known for rapid growth and excellent carcass qualities, highly popular worldwide for meat production.

Dairy Breed

Saanen Goat

The largest of the dairy goat breeds, producing exceptional milk volumes with a calm temperament.

Dual Purpose

Anglo-Nubian

Recognizable by their long drooping ears. Excellent dual-purpose breed rich in milk butterfat content.

Dairy Breed

Alpine Goat

Highly adaptable and heavy milk yielders. Known for curiosity, resilience, and unique colors.

Dairy Breed

Toggenburg

Oldest registered dairy breed. Features distinct swiss markings and steady, long-lasting milk production.

Meat Breed

Kalahari Red

Distinctly red-skinned, highly sun-resistant, hardy meat goat suited for rugged, dry conditions.

Fiber Breed

Angora Goat

Produces luxurious mohair fleece. Requires proper grooming and specialized care for best fiber.

Miniature / Pet

Nigerian Dwarf

Small-sized West African dairy goat. Exceptionally sweet milk, great family pets, and easy handling.

Dairy Breed

LaMancha

Famous for having uniquely short 'gopher' or 'elf' ears. Highly productive and affectionate dairy breed.

Meat Breed

Kiko Goat

Bred for survival and rapid growth under natural conditions without intensive management.

Meat / Dual

Jamunapari

An iconic Indian breed with a parrot-mouth appearance, tall stature, and solid multi-purpose value.

Meat Breed

Black Bengal

Highly prolific breed yielding top-grade meat and premium quality skin leather.

Dairy Breed

Oberhasli

A Swiss dairy breed featuring a gorgeous chamois colored coat with neat black markings.

Miniature Breed

Pygmy Goat

Compact, stocky, and incredibly playful. Primarily kept as companion animals and clearing brush.

Meat / Hardiness

Spanish Goat

A highly resilient landrace breed capable of thriving in rough, brushy terrain with minimal care.

Fiber Breed

Cashmere Goat

Produces down-soft cashmere wool beneath its outer hair. Valued deeply for winter shearing.

Meat Breed

Savanna Goat

Large, muscular, pure white goats from South Africa engineered for extreme drought endurance.

Rare Breed

Bagot Goat

One of the oldest semi-feral British breeds, beautiful with a black head and clean white body.

Dairy Breed

Guernsey Goat

Rare golden-hued dairy goat known for gentle behavior and premium fat-rich golden milk.

Meat / Local

Malabari Goat

Native to Southern India, highly prolific, regular twin/triplet births, excellent climate resilience.
